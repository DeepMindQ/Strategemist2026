   308→<section class="pillar-sec" id="piilar-sd">
   309→  <div class="container">
   310→     <div class="smart-comtent regular-content">
   311→    <h5><img src="images/dots.svg" alt="icon">Three Lanes</h5>
   312→    <h2>The Three Pillars of Our Execution System</h2>
   313→  </div>
   314→  <div class="row">
   315→    <div class="col-md-4">
   316→      <div class="table-pillar">
   317→      <h6>Data Foundations — The Architecture of Trust</h6>
   318→      <p>Before intelligence, there must be integrity. We design lake house-centric data estates that treat data as a product and governance as code.</p>
   319→      <div class="reg-right">
   320→        <ul>
   321→          <li><span>Reference Architecture: Medallion (Bronze/Silver/Gold) on Databricks or cloud-native analogs; Delta/Apache Iceberg tables; CDC pipelines (Fivetran/Debezium) with idempotent replay.</span></li>
   322→          <li><span>Data Contracts & Quality: Schema contracts at ingress, Great Expectations/Delta Live Tables expectations, anomaly detection on freshness/volume/velocity.</span></li>
   323→          <li><span>Catalog & Lineage: Centralized governance with Unity Catalog; column-level lineage, purpose-based access, per-use audit trails.</span></li>
   324→          <li><span>Privacy & Security: Fine-grained ABAC/RBAC, tokenization and format-preserving encryption for PII/PHI, differential privacy where applicable.</span></li>
   325→          <li><span>Interoperability: Semantic layers (dbt/BI) with metric definitions under version control, enabling consistent executive reporting and CFO-grade reconciliation.</span></li>
   326→          <li><span>Outcome: A single, defensible source of truth with provable data reliability, lower integration debt, and accelerated analytics re-use.</span></li>
   327→        </ul>
   328→      </div>
   329→    </div>
   330→    </div>
   331→    <div class="col-md-4">
   332→       <div class="table-pillar">
   333→      <h6>Applied AI & Automation — Intelligence in Motion</h6>
   334→      <p>We move AI from the lab to the line. Solutions are embedded into workflows with explicit guardrails, observability, and financial accountability.</p>
   335→      <div class="reg-right">
   336→        <ul>
   337→          <li><span>Patterns We Operationalize: RAG with vector stores, document provenance, and policy filters; task-specific AI Agents with bounded autonomy, tool-use policies, and human-in-the-loop checkpoints; classical ML for prediction, optimization, and forecasting with feature stores and drift controls.</span></li>
   338→          <li><span>MLOps at Scale: CI/CD for models and prompts; model registry with version pinning; offline/online feature parity; shadow, canary, and blue-green releases; automated rollback on guardrail breach.</span></li>
   339→          <li><span>Evaluation & Safety: Benchmark harnesses for accuracy, latency, bias, toxicity; prompt/chain unit tests; red-teaming; enterprise prompt firewalls; content and data-use policies enforced at runtime.</span></li>
   340→          <li><span>ROI Discipline: Each AI artifact ships with a benefit hypothesis, cash-flow instrumentation, and post-deployment value realization dashboards.</span></li>
   341→          <li><span>Outcome: AI that is auditable, governable, and economically positive—measured in throughput gained, cycle time reduced, and error budgets preserved.</span></li>
   342→        </ul>
   343→      </div>
   344→    </div>
   345→    </div>
   346→    <div class="col-md-4">
   347→       <div class="table-pillar">
   348→      <h6>Secure, Reliable Delivery — The Engine of Confidence</h6>
   349→      <p>Innovation must sit on resilient rails. We embed Cloud, SRE, and DevSecOps as first principles.</p>
