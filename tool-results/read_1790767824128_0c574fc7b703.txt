   300→### 6.4 Three Lanes / Three Pillars of Execution

   303→**Pillar 1 — Data Foundations — The Architecture of Trust**

   305→- Reference Architecture: Medallion (Bronze/Silver/Gold) on Databricks or cloud-native analogs; Delta/Apache Iceberg tables; CDC pipelines (Fivetran/Debezium) with idempotent replay.
